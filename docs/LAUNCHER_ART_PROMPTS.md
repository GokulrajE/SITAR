## Text for the screens

**SITAR**: System for Independent Task-oriented Assessment and Rehabilitation. *Touch. Think. Move.*

| Panel | Title | Text |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Infrared touch frame | Light feels every finger and board |
| 2 | Up to 10 touches | Use one finger or both hands |
| 3 | 3 boards, 6 objects | Real things to hold and place |
| 4 | 4 skills | Attention, memory, perception, planning |
| 5 | 11 games | Tap, drag, reach and place |
| 6 | Your progress | Games and minutes really played; before that: *Your first flower is waiting* |

| Skill card | Line | Games |
|---|---|---|
| Attention | Focus on what matters. | Focus Finder, Dosa Master, Butterfly Garden |
| Memory | Keep it in mind and recall it. | Memory Meadow, Melody Steps |
| Perception | Recognise sounds, shapes and objects. | Listen & Discover, Connect the Picture, Shape Match |
| Planning & Movement | Plan a path and follow it. | Ocean Rescue, Seed Quest, Sentinel Dash |

**About SITAR** (one page): SITAR is a large touch table with games for hand and mind. A slim frame of
invisible light feels your fingers. Each three-legged board has its own triangle, so the table knows
which one you placed. Play alone, with family or with a therapist, at your own pace.

*Practice activities, not a medical treatment. A therapist can help choose the right games and levels.*
